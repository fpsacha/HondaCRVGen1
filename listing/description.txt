[h1]Honda CRV (1st Gen, RD1)[/h1]
I really like the first-gen CRV and I really wanted one in Zomboid, so I made it. It's the 1997-2001 model with real-time 4WD, the side-hinged tailgate with the spare hanging off it, the picnic table hidden under the cargo floor, and a B20B that just won't quit.

[b]AI disclaimer:[/b] I used AI (Claude, by Anthropic) to help make this mod. I directed it and tested everything in game. It's still new, so expect a few rough edges, and let me know if something's broken.

[b]Requires:[/b] Build 42. No other mods needed.

[h2]The car[/h2]
[list]
[*] All eight factory colours, from Electron Blue Pearl to Naples Gold
[*] Fold the back seats flat for a long load floor, or sleep in it. Nobody can ride in the back while they're folded
[*] There's a storage well under the cargo floor. Lift the floor to get to it
[*] Take the picnic table out and set it up behind the tailgate
[*] Roof rack with cargo you can actually see, bull bar, window bars, spare cover
[*] An owner's manual in every glove box, with the last owner's notes in the margins
[/list]

[h2]Off-road[/h2]
[list]
[*] 3 inch lift with all-terrain tyres, on the stock alloys or black steelies. You can take it back off and you keep all your parts and whatever's in the trunk
[*] Find the kit as rare loot, build it at a workbench, or learn it from the "4x4 Off-Road: CRV Edition" magazine
[*] Snorkel, skid plate, steel rear bumper with a hitch, ladder up to the roof rack
[*] Jerry cans on the tailgate and in the roof rack, and they hold real fuel
[/list]

[h2]Armour[/h2]
[list]
[*] Bolt-on door plates, window mesh, arch guards and a rear glass plate. It's heavy bare steel and zombies can't reach through it
[*] Craft it, find it in garages or military and police loot, or learn it from a magazine
[/list]

[h2]Under the hood[/h2]
[list]
[*] A full engine bay done from photos of a real B20B. Open the hood and the prop rod swings up to hold it
[*] Proper headlights, and the tail lights light up when you brake
[*] Its own engine sound, horn and door sounds
[*] Its own damage, rust and blood textures
[*] Spawns in suburbs, on farm roads, in parking lots and in traffic jams
[*] Sandbox options for spawn rate, accessory chance, loot rarity, armour strength and engine volume
[*] Made with multiplayer in mind (I'm still testing it on dedicated servers, see below)
[/list]

[h2]Known issues[/h2]
[list]
[*] Version 0.3.0, still early. If you hit a bug, please post it with your console log
[*] Not tested on a dedicated server yet
[*] I just changed how the lifted version handles hard braking and turns, so tell me how it feels
[*] Damage dents are still soft blotches for now
[/list]
