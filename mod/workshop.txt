description=[h1]Honda CRV (1st Gen, RD1)[/h1]
description=I really like the first-gen CRV and I really wanted one in Zomboid, so I made it. It's the 1997-2001 model with real-time 4WD, the side-hinged tailgate with the spare hanging off it, the picnic table hidden under the cargo floor, and a B20B that just won't quit.
description=
description=[b]AI disclaimer:[/b] I used AI (Claude, by Anthropic) to help make this mod. I directed it and tested everything in game. It's still new, so expect a few rough edges, and let me know if something's broken.
description=
description=[b]Requires:[/b] Build 42. No other mods needed.
description=
description=[h2]New in 0.4.0[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Side awning[/b] you can bolt on and roll out to stay dry in the rain
description=[*] [b]A real bed[/b] when you fold the back seats, and a pillow helps you sleep
description=[*] [b]Your trunk load shows[/b] in the back of the car
description=[*] [b]New interior[/b] done from photos of a real RD1
description=[*] [b]Nicer picnic table[/b] and better performance all round
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]The car[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] All eight factory colours, from Electron Blue Pearl to Naples Gold
description=[*] An interior done from photos of a real RD1: striped seats with proper bolsters, the walk-through tray with cup holders, round heater knobs, the big airbag wheel and the four-gauge cluster
description=[*] Whatever you put in the trunk shows up back there: a duffel and a box at first, then a cooler, a gas can, a toolbox and bags as it fills up
description=[*] There's a storage well under the cargo floor. Lift the floor to get to it
description=[*] Roof rack with cargo you can actually see, bull bar, window bars, spare cover
description=[*] An owner's manual in every glove box, with the last owner's notes in the margins
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Off-road[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] 3 inch lift with all-terrain tyres, on the stock alloys or black steelies. You can take it back off and you keep all your parts and whatever's in the trunk
description=[*] Find the kit as rare loot, build it at a workbench, or learn it from the "4x4 Off-Road: CRV Edition" magazine
description=[*] Snorkel, skid plate, steel rear bumper with a hitch, ladder up to the roof rack
description=[*] Jerry cans on the tailgate and in the roof rack, and they hold real fuel
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Camping[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Bed:[/b] fold the back seats flat and it's made up with a foam pad, a sleeping bag and a pillow. Sleeping in it counts as a good bed. Leave a real pillow on it or in your seat and you sleep even better. Nobody can ride in the back while it's folded
description=[*] [b]Side awning:[/b] a roll-out canopy on the roof rail. Stand under it and the rain doesn't soak you, and you dry off like you would under a roof. It works in multiplayer for everyone standing under it
description=[*] Find the awning in camping stores and garages, or sew one yourself from 2 tarps, 2 metal pipes, rope and screws (Tailoring 3, Mechanics 1). Fit it like any car part with a wrench
description=[*] Roll it out from the car's menu when you're parked. Forget it and it rolls itself up when you drive off, but pull away fast and it tears
description=[*] [b]Picnic table:[/b] it lives under the cargo floor. Take it out and set it up anywhere: full size like the real one, grey moulded top and black legs, and you can put things on it
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Armour[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] Bolt-on door plates, window mesh, arch guards and a rear glass plate. It's heavy bare steel and zombies can't reach through it
description=[*] Craft it, find it in garages or military and police loot, or learn it from a magazine
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Under the hood[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] A full engine bay done from photos of a real B20B. Open the hood and the prop rod swings up to hold it
description=[*] Proper headlights, and the tail lights light up when you brake
description=[*] Its own engine sound, horn and door sounds
description=[*] Its own damage, rust and blood textures
description=[*] Spawns in suburbs, on farm roads, in parking lots and in traffic jams
description=[*] Sandbox options for spawn rate, accessory chance, loot rarity, armour strength and engine volume
description=[*] Made with multiplayer in mind
description=[*] Works with Project Viewpoint, in first and third person
description=[/list]
